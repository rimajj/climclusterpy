The per-cell trainers and their distance matrices
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The pieces behind the per-cell modes, for anyone tuning or extending them. They need
``torch`` and are loaded on first use:

* ``train_local_lmnn``, ``train_enhanced_local_lmnn``, ``train_local_mahalanobis_lmnn`` —
  the three per-cell trainers behind ``lmnn_mode="local"``, ``"enhanced"`` and
  ``"lowrank"``;
* ``compute_distance_matrix_local``, ``compute_local_mahalanobis_distance_matrix`` — the
  n × n distance matrix under a trained diagonal or low-rank network.

Their docstrings are the reference: ``help(climclusterpy.clustering.lmnn.train_local_lmnn)``.
