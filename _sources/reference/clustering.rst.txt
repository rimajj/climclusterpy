The per-cell trainer and its distance matrices
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

These functions sit behind the per-cell modes and are useful if you tune or extend them.
They need ``torch`` and load on first use:

* ``train_local_mahalanobis_lmnn``: the per-cell trainer behind ``lmnn_mode="lowrank"`` and
  ``"diagonal"`` (its ``metric_type`` argument);
* ``compute_distance_matrix_local`` and ``compute_local_mahalanobis_distance_matrix``: the
  n × n distance matrix under a trained diagonal or low-rank network.

Their docstrings are the reference. For example, run ``help(climclusterpy.clustering.lmnn.train_local_mahalanobis_lmnn)``.
