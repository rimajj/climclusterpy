Supervision labels
==================

LMNN class labels come from three sources, and all three produce the same
``cell_id, latitude, longitude, cluster_label`` table. :func:`~climclusterpy.compute_supervision`
is the easiest way to reach them. The generators below, all importable from
``climclusterpy.supervision``, give finer control.

.. py:module:: climclusterpy.supervision

``KG_LABELS`` maps each of the 30 Köppen–Geiger class codes to its name (``"Af"``,
``"BWh"``, ...), and ``LABEL_TO_CODE`` is the reverse.
